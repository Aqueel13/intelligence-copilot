# Three-Way Matching and Exceptions

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. The match

1.1 Every invoice is matched against its **purchase order** (price, terms) and the **goods receipt note** (GRN; quantity received) before it can be approved for payment. Service invoices are matched against a service entry sheet, which counts as the receipt.

## 2. Tolerances

2.1 **Price:** the invoice unit price may differ from the PO price by at most **1% or ₹500 per line, whichever is lower**.

2.2 **Quantity:** the invoiced quantity may not exceed the quantity received.

2.3 Tolerances are set by the Finance Controller. Wider tolerances weaken a key control and need CFO approval.

## 3. GRN pending

3.1 If no GRN exists when the invoice reaches matching, the invoice is parked as **GRN pending** and the plant's stores team is notified automatically.

3.2 The invoice is matched again as soon as the GRN is posted (WH-201).

## 4. Price exceptions

4.1 An invoice outside the price tolerance is a **price exception**. It is routed to the buyer who raised the PO.

4.2 The buyer resolves it within **2 working days** by amending the PO (if the new price was agreed) or obtaining a credit note from the supplier. The invoice is then matched again.

4.3 Price exceptions are rare on catalogue and contract POs, which carry an agreed price. Most exceptions come from **free-text POs**, where the requester types an estimated price (see PRC-101).

## 5. Reporting

5.1 The price-exception rate is reported monthly by PO type and spend category.
