# Plant Profiles and Supplier Base

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Plants

| Plant | Products | Share of invoices | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chakan (Pune) | Brake assemblies, machining | about 24% | Largest inbound deliveries; GRN staffing see WH-201 |
| Hosur | Steering components | about 22% | |
| Manesar | Castings | about 22% | |
| Sanand | Fabricated brackets and sub-assemblies | about 18% | Large local job-work supplier base |
| Sriperumbudur | Electronics sub-assemblies | about 14% | |

## 2. Supplier base by plant

2.1 Chakan, Hosur, Manesar and Sriperumbudur buy mainly from established suppliers who submit through the supplier portal or by e-mail.

2.2 **Sanand** relies on a cluster of **local job-work and fabrication shops**. Most of them still send **paper invoices** to the plant gate; about three in five Sanand invoices arrive on paper, against about one in ten elsewhere.

## 3. Implications for accounts payable

3.1 Invoice handling time depends on the invoice channel more than on the plant: paper invoices wait for courier and central scanning (AP-104 §4).

3.2 When comparing plants, compare **within each invoice channel** first.
