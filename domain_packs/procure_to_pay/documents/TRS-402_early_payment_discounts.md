# Early Payment Discounts

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Lets the company pay selected suppliers early in return for a discount, when cash allows.

## 2. Terms

2.1 Suppliers may opt in to a **dynamic discount** of up to **1.5%** for payment within 10 days of invoice receipt, reducing in line with the days saved.

2.2 Only invoices that are fully approved can be offered. Early payment does not bypass matching or approval.

## 3. Interaction with the payment run calendar

3.1 Discounted invoices are paid in the normal payment runs (TRS-401). An extra run is not created for them.

3.2 When the payment run calendar is reduced (TRS-401 §3), fewer invoices qualify for a discount.

## 4. Approval

The Treasury Head decides which suppliers are offered discounts and suspends the programme when cash requires it.
