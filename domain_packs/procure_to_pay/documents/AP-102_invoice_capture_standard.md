# Invoice Capture Standard

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Channels

1.1 Suppliers submit invoices through the **supplier portal** (structured e-invoice, no manual capture needed for header data), by **e-mail** (PDF) or on **paper** at the plant gate.

1.2 Large suppliers mostly use the portal. MSME suppliers mostly send PDF or paper invoices.

## 2. Who captures

2.1 **AP Pune** and **AP Chennai** (in-house) capture portal and e-mail invoices for their region's plants.

2.2 The **BPO Partner** (outsourced since October 2025) captures all paper invoices and overflow e-mail invoices, for all plants.

## 3. Capture steps

3.1 Capture header data (supplier, invoice number and date, GSTIN, totals, PO number) and line items against the PO.

3.2 Capture within **1 working day** of receipt (AP-001 §4.1).

## 4. Capture accuracy

4.1 A capture error is any field that makes the three-way match fail and is corrected by re-capturing the invoice (wrong PO number, transposed quantities, wrong tax split).

4.2 Target: at least **97% first-time-right**, i.e. no more than **3%** of invoices re-captured. Each re-capture sends the invoice to the back of the capture queue and typically adds two working days.

## 5. Quality management

5.1 Re-capture rates are reported monthly by AP team.

5.2 **BPO Partner contract.** If the BPO Partner's re-capture rate exceeds **6%** for **two consecutive months**, the AP Manager triggers the quality plan: targeted retraining on the error types found, and 100% second-check of the BPO Partner's captures for 30 days. Service credits under the contract apply from the third month.

5.3 The AP Manager owns the quality plan and approves its activation.
