# Purchasing Policy: Purchase Order Types

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. PO types

1.1 **Catalogue PO:** an item from the approved catalogue, at the catalogue price.

1.2 **Contract PO:** a call-off against a rate contract (agreed prices for a period).

1.3 **Free-text PO:** a description and an estimated price typed by the requester, with no agreed price.

## 2. Default

2.1 Direct materials (raw materials, packaging) are bought on contract or catalogue POs.

2.2 Indirect spend (MRO, logistics, IT and professional services) should also use catalogue or contract POs wherever a catalogue item or rate contract exists.

## 3. When a free-text PO is allowed

3.1 A free-text PO is allowed only for a genuine **one-off purchase below ₹2,00,000**, or in an emergency approved by the plant head.

3.2 Because the price is an estimate, free-text POs are the main source of invoice price exceptions (AP-103 §4.3).

## 4. Repeat purchases

4.1 A service or item bought on free-text POs **three or more times in 12 months** must be moved to a rate contract or catalogue item within **90 days**.

4.2 Procurement may block free-text POs for categories where a catalogue or rate contract exists (a setting in the SRM portal).

## 5. Ownership

5.1 The Head of Procurement owns PO-type compliance and approves changes to the SRM portal's PO rules.

## 6. Internal audit finding (FY2025-26)

6.1 Internal audit found that **IT services and professional services** were still bought mostly on free-text POs, many of them repeat purchases that should have been on rate contracts under §4.1.
