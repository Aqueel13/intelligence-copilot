# Invoice Approval Authority

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Who approves

1.1 Every matched invoice is approved by the **budget holder** of the cost centre on the PO.

1.2 Invoices above **₹10,00,000** additionally need the **Finance Controller**'s approval.

## 2. Targets

2.1 Budget holders approve within **2 working days** of the invoice being matched.

2.2 The Finance Controller approves within **1 working day**.

## 3. Absence and delegation

3.1 A budget holder who is away for more than two working days must delegate approval in the ERP to a named deputy.

3.2 The Finance Controller's authority may be delegated to the Deputy Finance Controller for invoices up to ₹50,00,000.

## 4. What approval confirms

4.1 Approval confirms that the goods or services were needed and that the cost centre is correct. It does not re-check price or quantity, which the three-way match has already done (AP-103).
