# Payments to MSME Suppliers

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional). Internal summary; not legal advice.

## 1. Who is an MSME supplier

1.1 A supplier registered as a micro, small or medium enterprise (Udyam registration) is flagged "MSME" in the supplier master. The flag is maintained by Procurement on onboarding and re-checked every April.

1.2 About 40% of our suppliers are MSMEs, concentrated in packaging, MRO and logistics.

## 2. Statutory payment terms

2.1 Under the MSMED Act, 2006 (section 15), micro and small enterprises must be paid on or before the agreed date, and the agreed period may not exceed **45 days** from acceptance of the goods or services. Where there is no written agreement, payment is due within **15 days**.

2.2 Late payment attracts compound interest at three times the bank rate notified by the Reserve Bank of India (section 16).

2.3 For income-tax purposes, payments to micro and small enterprises made after the section 15 limit are deductible only in the year they are actually paid (Income-tax Act, section 43B(h)).

2.4 Amounts outstanding to micro and small enterprises for more than 45 days are reported half-yearly to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MSME Form 1).

## 3. Our commitment

3.1 MSME invoices follow the same **10-day** invoice-to-pay target as all other invoices (AP-001 §3). There is no separate, slower track.

3.2 MSME suppliers often send paper or PDF invoices. These are captured by the BPO Partner under AP-102 and must meet the same capture accuracy standard.

3.3 An MSME supplier may request an off-cycle payment if an invoice is at risk of passing 30 days (TRS-401 §5).

## 4. Monitoring

4.1 The on-time payment rate for MSME suppliers is reported monthly to the CFO alongside the overall breach rate.
