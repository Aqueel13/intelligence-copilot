# Invoice-to-Pay Service Levels

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets how quickly supplier invoices are processed and paid, and how the target is monitored. It applies to all five plants (Chakan, Hosur, Manesar, Sanand, Sriperumbudur) and all spend categories.

## 2. Why the target matters

2.1 **Early-payment discounts.** About a third of our suppliers offer a 2% discount for payment within 10 days of invoice ("2/10 net 45"). Every invoice paid after day 10 loses that discount.

2.2 **MSME suppliers.** Payments to micro and small enterprises are governed by the MSMED Act (see AP-002). Paying within 10 days keeps us well inside the statutory limit.

2.3 **Supplier relationships.** Late payment is the most common reason suppliers give for refusing to hold prices.

## 3. Invoice-to-pay target

3.1 Every invoice is paid within **10 calendar days** of the day it is received (portal upload, e-mail or paper), measured from receipt to payment release.

3.2 Time spent waiting for a goods receipt, a price correction or an approval counts towards the 10 days. There is no "clock stop".

## 4. Internal stage targets

4.1 Invoice capture: within **1 working day** of receipt (AP-102).

4.2 Three-way match: the same working day as capture.

4.3 Goods receipt (GRN) posting: within **1 working day** of unloading (WH-201). An invoice that reaches matching before its GRN is "GRN pending".

4.4 Price exceptions: resolved by the buyer within **2 working days** (AP-103).

4.5 Budget holder approval: within **2 working days**; Finance Controller approval (invoices above ₹10,00,000) within **1 working day** (APR-301).

4.6 Payment: in the next scheduled payment run (TRS-401).

## 5. Monitoring

5.1 The invoice-to-pay breach rate (share of invoices paid after day 10) is reported monthly by plant, spend category and AP team.

5.2 A breach rate above **25%** in a month is a warning; above **30%** is critical and must be reviewed by the Head of Accounts Payable with Procurement and Treasury within two weeks.

5.3 Any stage that misses its target for **two consecutive months** is escalated to the stage owner's head of function.
