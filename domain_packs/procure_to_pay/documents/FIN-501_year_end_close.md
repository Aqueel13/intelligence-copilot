# Financial Year-End Close Calendar

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Sets the accounts payable steps for closing the financial year on **31 March**.

## 2. Expected volumes

2.1 Suppliers bill outstanding work before the year end, so invoices received in **March** run about **40% above** a normal month.

2.2 Accounts payable staffing is not increased for March; overtime is approved instead.

## 3. Cut-offs

3.1 Invoices received by **25 March** are captured and accrued in the closing year. Later invoices are accrued on the basis of goods receipts.

3.2 The payment run calendar (TRS-401) is unchanged in March.

## 4. Year-end review

The AP Manager reports invoices outstanding at year end, by age and by supplier size, to the Chief Financial Officer by 10 April. MSME invoices older than 45 days are reported separately (AP-002).
