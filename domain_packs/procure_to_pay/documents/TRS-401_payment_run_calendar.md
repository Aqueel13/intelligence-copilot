# Payment Run Calendar

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Approved invoices are paid in scheduled payment runs, so Treasury can plan cash and send one bank file per run.

## 2. Standard calendar

2.1 Payment runs take place every **Tuesday and Friday at 11:00 IST**.

2.2 **Cut-off:** an invoice is included in a run if it is fully approved by **17:00 IST on the previous working day**.

2.3 With two runs a week an approved invoice waits at most about four and a half days for payment.

## 3. Change log

3.1 **From 1 July 2026 the Tuesday run is suspended** as an interim cash-management measure (CFO memo FIN-27-014). Until it is restored, payment runs take place on **Fridays only**, and an invoice that misses Thursday's cut-off waits a full week.

3.2 The suspension was to be reviewed after one quarter.

## 4. Changes to the calendar

4.1 Adding, suspending or restoring a payment run requires approval by the **Treasury Head** or the **Chief Financial Officer**.

4.2 Each additional run costs about four hours of Treasury time and brings payments forward, which has a working-capital cost.

## 5. Off-cycle payments

5.1 Off-cycle payments are allowed for MSME invoices at risk of passing 30 days (AP-002 §3.3) and for statutory payments.
