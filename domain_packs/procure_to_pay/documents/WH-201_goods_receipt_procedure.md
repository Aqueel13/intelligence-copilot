# Goods Receipt Procedure

Varaha Auto Components Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Scope

1.1 This procedure covers goods deliveries (raw materials, packaging, MRO) at all plants. Services are confirmed by the requester (service entry sheet), not by stores.

## 2. Posting the goods receipt

2.1 Each plant's stores team checks the delivery against the PO and posts the **goods receipt note (GRN)** in the ERP.

2.2 The GRN is what releases the invoice for matching: an invoice without a GRN waits as "GRN pending" (AP-103 §3).

## 3. Posting target

3.1 GRNs are posted within **1 working day (24 hours)** of unloading.

3.2 Deliveries that fail quality inspection are receipted into blocked stock within the same day; they are not left unposted.

## 4. Staffing

4.1 Each plant has one stores clerk per shift responsible for GRN posting. Chakan, the largest plant by inbound deliveries, has two.

4.2 Chakan lost one of its two GRN clerks in March 2025; the position has not been back-filled (hiring freeze on indirect staff).

## 5. Escalation and improvement

5.1 The **Plant Head** owns GRN posting compliance at the plant.

5.2 If a plant's average wait for GRN posting exceeds the target for **two consecutive months**, the Plant Head must either enforce same-day posting (daily cut-off check, gate-in scan) or request additional stores capacity.

5.3 **Gate-in scan.** Hosur piloted posting the GRN from the gate-in scan for catalogue and contract deliveries in 2025; GRN waits there fell below 8 hours. Rolling it out to another plant needs Plant Head approval and a WMS change request.
