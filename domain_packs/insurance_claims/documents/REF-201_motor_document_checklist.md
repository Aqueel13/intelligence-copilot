# Motor Claim Document Checklist (reimbursement)

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## Mandatory documents

1. Duly filled and signed claim form.
2. Copy of the policy schedule.
3. Copy of the vehicle Registration Certificate (RC).
4. Copy of the driving licence of the person driving at the time of the accident.
5. Repair estimate from the garage.
6. Final repair invoice and payment receipt (for reimbursement).
7. Photographs of the damaged vehicle.
8. Cancelled cheque or bank details for NEFT payment.
9. KYC documents of the insured (PAN and address proof).

## Conditional documents

- **FIR copy**: for theft, third-party injury or major accidents.
- **Panchnama**: where police recorded one.
- **Fitness certificate and permit**: for commercial vehicles.
- **Loan NOC from financier**: where the vehicle is hypothecated and the claim is a total loss.

## Common reasons for additional-documents requests

The documents most often missing from motor claims are the driving licence copy, the final repair invoice, and the cancelled cheque.
