# Broker Partner Claims Submission Guidelines

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Sets out what the Company expects from broker partners when they submit claims on behalf of policyholders, and how submission quality is monitored.

## 2. Broker responsibilities

2.1 The broker must collect **all documents on the applicable checklist** (REF-201 motor, REF-202 health) before submitting the claim.

2.2 The broker must use the broker portal's checklist upload, which marks each item as provided or not applicable.

2.3 The broker remains the customer's point of contact for any additional-documents request on a claim they submitted.

## 3. Submission quality

3.1 **First-time-right (FTR) rate**: the share of a broker's claims that pass the Company's document check without an additional-documents request. The target is **at least 92%**.

3.2 FTR is reported to each broker monthly, together with the most common missing documents.

3.3 Brokers below 85% FTR for two consecutive months are placed on a quality improvement plan, which includes a refresher session on the checklists and a mandatory pre-submission review for 60 days.

## 4. Known issues

The broker portal's checklist upload is optional in the current release (portal version 3.1), so some brokers submit claims without completing it. Making the checklist mandatory is on the portal roadmap but has no committed date.

## 5. Customer impact

Additional-documents requests on broker claims reach the customer through the broker, which adds delay. Customers frequently report not knowing what was missing.
