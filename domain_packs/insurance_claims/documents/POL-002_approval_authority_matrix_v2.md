# Claims Approval Authority Matrix

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional) · Version 2.1

## 1. Purpose

Defines who may give final approval for a claim settlement, by claim amount, and the turnaround expected at each level.

## 2. Change summary (version 2.0, April 2026)

Following the internal audit of FY2025-26, the threshold for mandatory senior adjuster approval was **lowered from ₹5,00,000 to ₹2,00,000** with effect from 1 April 2026, to strengthen control over mid-value claims. Version 2.1 corrected formatting only.

## 3. Approval levels

| Claim amount | Final approver | Turnaround target |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹2,00,000 | Claims Officer, after QA pass | same working day |
| Above ₹2,00,000 up to ₹25,00,000 | Senior Adjuster | **within 24 hours of QA pass** |
| Above ₹25,00,000 | Head of Claims | within 2 working days |

## 4. Rules

4.1 A claim may not be settled until the approver at the required level has recorded approval in the claims system.

4.2 Approvers may not approve claims they verified themselves.

4.3 **Delegation.** Senior Adjuster authority for claims **above ₹2,00,000 and up to ₹5,00,000** may be delegated to **Team Leads** with at least five years of claims experience, subject to written approval by the Head of Claims. Delegated approvals are subject to a monthly 10% sample audit by the Senior Adjuster pool. *As of the effective date of this version, this delegation has not been invoked.*

4.4 Claims above ₹5,00,000 may not be delegated.

## 5. Monitoring

The waiting time before Senior Approval is reported monthly. If the average wait exceeds the 24-hour target for two consecutive months, the Head of Claims must review approver capacity or invoke the delegation in 4.3.
