# Health Claim Document Checklist (reimbursement)

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## Mandatory documents

1. Duly filled and signed claim form (Part A by the insured, Part B by the hospital).
2. Original discharge summary.
3. Final hospital bill with itemised break-up and payment receipt.
4. Pharmacy bills with prescriptions.
5. Investigation and diagnostic reports.
6. Doctor's first consultation note recommending hospitalisation.
7. Cancelled cheque or bank details for NEFT payment.
8. KYC documents of the proposer (PAN and address proof).

## Conditional documents

- **FIR or medico-legal certificate (MLC)**: for accident-related hospitalisation.
- **Implant sticker or invoice**: where an implant was used.
- **Death summary**: in case of death.

## Common reasons for additional-documents requests

The documents most often missing from health claims are the itemised bill break-up, the doctor's first consultation note, and investigation reports.
