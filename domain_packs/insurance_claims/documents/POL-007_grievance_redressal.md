# Customer Grievance Redressal Policy

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Channels

Customers may complain by phone, e-mail, the website or at any branch, and may escalate to the Bima Bharosa portal of IRDAI.

## 2. Timelines

2.1 Every complaint is **acknowledged within 3 days** of receipt.

2.2 Complaints are **resolved within 14 days**. If not, the customer is told the reason and may approach the Insurance Ombudsman.

## 3. Claims-related complaints

3.1 A complaint about a delayed claim is routed to the claims team that holds the claim, with a copy to the Claims Operations Manager.

3.2 Claims that have breached the five-day turnaround (POL-001) are given priority at every remaining stage once a complaint is received.

## 4. Learning from complaints

The Grievance Redressal Officer reports the top complaint themes monthly, alongside the post-closure survey results (POL-005).
