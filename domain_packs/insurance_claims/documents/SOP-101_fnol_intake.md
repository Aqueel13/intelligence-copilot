# FNOL and Claim Intake Procedure

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Ensures every claim is registered correctly at First Notice of Loss (FNOL) and that the document check is completed once, completely, so the claim can move straight to assessment.

## 2. FNOL registration

2.1 Claims are notified through the customer app, the contact centre (24x7), brokers or agents.

2.2 The Contact Centre registers FNOL in the claims system and gives the customer a claim reference.

2.3 At FNOL, the customer (or the broker or agent acting for them) must be given the **document checklist** for the line of business (REF-201 for motor, REF-202 for health) in writing.

## 3. Document check

3.1 The Intake Desk checks the submitted documents against the checklist **within 4 working hours** of FNOL.

3.2 **The checklist applies to every channel.** Claims submitted by brokers and agents are checked to the same standard as direct claims; the intermediary is responsible for submitting a complete pack.

3.3 If documents are missing, the Intake Desk must raise **one consolidated request** listing every missing item. Raising a second request for an item that could have been identified at the first check is recorded as an intake quality failure.

3.4 Time spent waiting for the customer counts towards the 5-day SLA (POL-001 §3.3).

## 4. Quality measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Claims needing an additional-documents request | below 8% for every channel |
| Claims needing a second request | below 1% |
| Document check within 4 working hours | 95% |

## 5. Escalation

If a broker's claims repeatedly arrive incomplete, the Intake Desk Lead must raise it with the Broker Relationship team under the Broker Partner Claims Submission Guidelines (SOP-102).
