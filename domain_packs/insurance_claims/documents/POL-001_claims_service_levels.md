# Claims Service Level Policy

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional) · Claims Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the turnaround time (TAT) the Company commits to for reimbursement claims under motor and health policies, the internal targets for each stage of the claim, and how performance is measured and reported.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all reimbursement claims notified through any channel (direct, broker, agent). Cashless health claims and pre-authorisation are covered by a separate policy and are out of scope.

## 3. Customer turnaround commitment

3.1 Every reimbursement claim must be **settled and closed within 5 calendar days (120 hours) of First Notice of Loss (FNOL)**. This is the Claims Turnaround SLA.

3.2 The SLA clock starts at FNOL registration and stops when the claim is marked Closed after payment.

3.3 Time spent waiting for the customer to provide additional documents **is not excluded** from the SLA clock. Avoiding repeated document requests is therefore an SLA obligation, not only a customer-experience goal.

## 4. Internal stage targets

To meet the 5-day commitment, each stage has an internal target measured in working hours (Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST):

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Document Check after FNOL | within 4 working hours |
| Survey & Loss Assessment (motor) | surveyor appointed within 1 working day |
| Medical Adjudication (health) | completed within 1 working day |
| Verification | within 1 working day of assessment |
| QA review | within 4 working hours of verification |
| Senior Approval (claims above ₹2,00,000) | **within 24 hours** of QA pass |
| Settlement | payment released within 1 working day of final approval |

## 5. Measurement and reporting

5.1 SLA compliance is measured as the share of claims closed within 120 hours of FNOL, reported monthly by line of business, channel and region.

5.2 The monthly Claims Operations Review must include the five stages with the longest average waiting time and any stage whose waiting time exceeds its target for two consecutive months.

5.3 Breach rates for groups with fewer than 100 claims in a month must be reported with a small-sample caveat and must not be used alone to drive staffing or partner decisions.

## 6. Breaches

Claims at risk of breaching are escalated according to the Claims Escalation Matrix (POL-004). Persistent breaches at a single stage require a corrective action plan from the stage owner within 15 working days.
