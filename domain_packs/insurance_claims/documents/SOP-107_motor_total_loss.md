# Motor Total Loss and Salvage Procedure

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. When a vehicle is a total loss

1.1 A vehicle is treated as a **constructive total loss** when the surveyor's assessed repair cost exceeds **75% of the insured declared value (IDV)**.

1.2 Flood-damaged vehicles with water above the dashboard are assessed for total loss first.

## 2. Process

2.1 The total-loss decision is made in the survey report (SOP-103). Survey timelines are unchanged.

2.2 The customer signs the transfer documents and hands over the registration certificate and keys; the salvage is sold through the empanelled salvage buyer.

2.3 Settlement is the IDV less the policy excess, paid after the documents are complete. The turnaround commitment in POL-001 applies from the date the documents are complete.

## 3. Approvals

Total-loss settlements follow the approval levels in POL-002 according to the settlement amount.
