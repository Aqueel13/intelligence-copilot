# Claims Approval Authority Matrix

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional) · Version 1.0 · **SUPERSEDED on 1 April 2026**

## 1. Approval levels

| Claim amount | Final approver | Turnaround target |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹5,00,000 | Claims Officer, after QA pass | same working day |
| Above ₹5,00,000 up to ₹25,00,000 | Senior Adjuster | within 2 working days |
| Above ₹25,00,000 | Head of Claims | within 3 working days |

## 2. Rules

2.1 A claim may not be settled until the approver at the required level has recorded approval.

2.2 There is no provision for delegating Senior Adjuster authority.
