# Claims Escalation Matrix

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Claim-level escalation

| Elapsed since FNOL | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 72 hours, not yet approved | Alert to the current stage's Team Lead | Claims system |
| 96 hours, not yet settled | Escalation to the Claims Operations Manager | Team Lead |
| 120 hours (SLA breached) | Breach recorded; customer called with a revised date | Claims Operations Manager |
| 168 hours | Escalation to the Head of Claims | Claims Operations Manager |

## 2. Stage-level escalation

2.1 A stage whose average waiting time exceeds its target (POL-001 §4) for **two consecutive months** is escalated to the Head of Claims Operations, who must approve a corrective action plan within 15 working days.

2.2 Corrective actions may include rebalancing work between teams, overtime, invoking delegation under POL-002 §4.3, or activating the weekend rota under POL-003 §3.

## 3. Customer complaints

Complaints about delay are logged in the grievance system and must be acknowledged within 24 hours. Repeated complaints for the same claim are escalated to the Grievance Officer.
