# Health Claim Medical Adjudication Procedure

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Describes how reimbursement health claims are medically reviewed and how the payable amount is decided.

## 2. Adjudication

2.1 The Medical Team (in-house doctors, or the Third Party Administrator (TPA) where the policy uses one) reviews the discharge summary, diagnosis, bills and investigation reports.

2.2 The adjudicator confirms the treatment is covered, applies room-rent and other sub-limits, co-payment and deductibles, and records the admissible amount.

2.3 Adjudication must be completed **within 1 working day** of a complete document set being available.

## 3. Queries

3.1 Medical queries to the hospital or customer must be raised in a single consolidated query.

3.2 A medical query is not the same as an additional-documents request; it is recorded separately.

## 4. Claim value

Health reimbursement claims are on average higher in value than motor claims, so a larger share of them need Senior Adjuster approval under the Approval Authority Matrix (POL-002). Adjudication itself takes a similar time for both lines.
