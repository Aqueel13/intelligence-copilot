# Claim Settlement and Payment Procedure

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Settlement

1.1 Finance Ops releases payment by NEFT **within 1 working day** of final approval being recorded.

1.2 Payment is made to the bank account verified during intake. Payment to a third party (for example a garage) needs the insured's written consent.

## 2. Closure

2.1 The claim is marked **Closed** in the claims system once the payment is confirmed. This stops the SLA clock.

2.2 The customer receives a settlement letter by email and SMS with the amount paid and any deductions.

## 3. Failed payments

If a payment fails (for example because of incorrect bank details), Finance Ops contacts the customer and re-attempts within 1 working day of receiving corrected details. The claim stays open until payment succeeds.
