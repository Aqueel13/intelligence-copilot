# Claims Fraud Referral Policy

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Scope

All motor and health reimbursement claims. Applies to every team that handles a claim.

## 2. Red flags

2.1 Staff must refer a claim to the **Special Investigations Unit (SIU)** if any of these is found:
- an accident or admission within 30 days of policy inception or of a sum-insured increase;
- documents that appear altered, or bills from a provider on the watch list;
- a repair estimate more than 40% above the surveyor's assessment;
- repeated claims from the same garage or hospital for unrelated customers within a month.

2.2 Referral is made in the claims system with the reason; it does not need a manager's approval.

## 3. Handling of referred claims

3.1 Referred claims leave the normal process and are handled by the SIU. They are **reported separately** and excluded from turnaround reporting under POL-001 while under investigation.

3.2 Fewer than 1% of claims are referred in a typical month.

3.3 The customer is told that additional verification is required, without disclosing the reason.

## 4. Outcomes

If the SIU clears the claim, it returns to Verification with priority and the turnaround clock restarts from the date it is cleared. Repudiation needs the Head of Claims and the Head of Special Investigations.
