# Customer Communication and Feedback Policy

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Status updates

Customers receive SMS and email updates at FNOL registration, when documents are requested, at approval, and at settlement.

## 2. Post-closure survey

2.1 A satisfaction survey is sent **1 to 3 days after the claim is closed**.

2.2 The survey asks for:
- **CSAT**: "How satisfied are you with how your claim was handled?" on a 1–5 scale.
- **NPS**: "How likely are you to recommend Kestrel to a friend?" on a 0–10 scale.
- An optional free-text comment.

## 3. Reporting

3.1 CSAT is reported as the average score of respondents. NPS is the percentage of promoters (9–10) minus the percentage of detractors (0–6).

3.2 **Response bias.** Survey response is voluntary. Customers with a poor experience are more likely to respond, so respondent averages tend to understate satisfaction across all customers. Reports must state the number of responses and the response rate alongside any score.

3.3 Scores based on fewer than 30 responses must not be reported for a segment.

## 4. Use of comments

Free-text comments are reviewed monthly for recurring themes, such as repeated document requests, waiting for approval, or lack of updates. Themes are shared with the owners of the relevant stages.
