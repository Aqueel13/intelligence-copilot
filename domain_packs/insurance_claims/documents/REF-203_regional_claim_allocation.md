# Regional Allocation of Claims to Verification Teams

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Explains how claims are allocated to Verification Teams A, B and C after assessment.

## 2. Default allocation

2.1 Claims are allocated **round-robin across Teams A, B and C**, whatever the region, so workloads stay even.

2.2 Allocation does not depend on line of business, channel or claim amount.

## 3. Eastern hub exception

3.1 Since January 2025 **Team B works from the Kolkata office** as the eastern hub. Claims from the **Kolkata region** are allocated to Team B where capacity allows, so local hospital and garage queries can be handled in the same time zone and language. In practice about **four in five** Kolkata claims go to Team B.

3.2 Team B also takes its normal round-robin share of claims from every other region.

## 4. Implications for reporting

4.1 Because of §3, Kolkata's results largely reflect Team B's results. Compare regions **within each team** before drawing conclusions about a region.

4.2 Team quality metrics (QA rejection, rework) are owned by Quality Assurance (SOP-105).
