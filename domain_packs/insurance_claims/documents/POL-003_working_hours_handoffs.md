# Claims Operations Working Hours and Handoff Policy

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Working hours

1.1 Back-office claims teams (Intake Desk, Verification Teams, QA Desk, Senior Adjusters, Finance Ops) work **Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 IST**.

1.2 There is **no weekend coverage** for back-office teams. The Contact Centre registers FNOL 24x7.

## 2. Handoffs between teams

2.1 Work handed to the next team before 15:00 should be picked up the same working day.

2.2 **Friday cut-off.** Work handed over after **15:00 on Friday** is not allocated until the next working day. It joins the Monday morning backlog, which is typically cleared by mid-day Monday.

2.3 Because the SLA is measured in calendar days (POL-001), a claim that misses the Friday cut-off at any stage usually loses close to three calendar days.

## 3. Weekend rota (optional)

3.1 With approval of the Chief Operating Officer, a **Saturday half-day rota (09:00–13:00)** may be activated for the Verification Teams, QA Desk and Finance Ops to clear Friday handoffs.

3.2 The rota has not been activated in FY2026-27.

## 4. Public holidays

Public holidays follow the national and state holiday list published each January. Holidays are treated like weekends for handoffs.
