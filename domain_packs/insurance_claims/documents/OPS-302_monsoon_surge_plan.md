# Monsoon Surge Plan for Motor Claims

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Keeps motor claims within the five-day turnaround (POL-001) during the south-west monsoon, when accidents, waterlogging and flood damage raise motor claim volumes sharply.

## 2. Expected volumes

2.1 Based on the last three seasons, motor FNOLs from **1 July to 30 September** run about **60% above** the monthly average for the rest of the year. Health volumes are not seasonal.

2.2 The Surveyor Network is sized for normal months. At monsoon volumes, surveyor appointment slips from the one-working-day target in SOP-103 §2.1 to two or three working days unless extra capacity is added.

## 3. Surge panel

3.1 A **surge panel** of 12 independent surveyors and loss assessors licensed by IRDAI is pre-empanelled each year, with agreed fees, so it can start within one week of activation.

3.2 **Video surveys** may be used for motor claims up to ₹1,50,000 during the surge, as SOP-103 §2.2 permits.

3.3 Activation triggers (any one):
- average wait for surveyor appointment above **24 hours for five consecutive working days**;
- the IMD forecast of a heavy-rainfall spell in two or more major regions;
- 1 July, if the Claims Operations Manager expects a normal or above-normal monsoon.

## 4. Authority and cost

4.1 The **Claims Operations Manager** activates and stands down the surge panel. Activation beyond the approved budget needs the **Head of Claims**.

4.2 Budget: about **₹9,00,000 per season** (retainer plus per-survey fees above the network rate).

## 5. Status

5.1 The surge panel was **not activated in the 2025 monsoon**: the budget was released only in October 2025, after the season.

5.2 Activation for the 2026 monsoon has not yet been requested.

## 6. Monitoring

Wait for surveyor appointment is reported weekly in the operations review from 15 June to 15 October, by region.
