# Claims Operations Capacity Plan FY2026-27

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Basis of the plan

This plan was prepared in February 2026 using FY2025-26 volumes and the **Approval Authority Matrix version 1.0** (senior approval required above ₹5,00,000).

## 2. Expected volumes

- Reimbursement claims: about 830 per month (motor about 55%, health about 45%).
- Claims requiring Senior Adjuster approval: **about 45 per month** (claims above ₹5,00,000).

## 3. Staffing

| Role | Headcount (FTE) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Intake Desk | 4 | FNOL and document check |
| Verification Teams A, B, C | 5 each | Team B includes 3 staff hired in Q4 FY2025-26 |
| QA Desk | 3 | |
| Senior Adjusters | **2** | Also responsible for large-loss handling, litigation support and audits |
| Finance Ops (settlement) | 2 | |

## 4. Senior Adjuster capacity

Approval work is planned at roughly 25% of Senior Adjuster time, which supports **about 2 approvals per adjuster per working day, or roughly 85 approvals per month** for the pool. Planned demand (45 per month) is well within capacity.

## 5. Risks

- A change in approval thresholds would change Senior Adjuster demand. **The plan does not yet reflect any revision of the Approval Authority Matrix.**
- New joiners in Team B are expected to reach full productivity and quality by mid-2026.
