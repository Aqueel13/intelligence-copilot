# Claim Verification and Quality Assurance Procedure

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Defines how assessed claims are verified by the verification teams and reviewed by the QA Desk before approval.

## 2. Verification

2.1 Claims are allocated to Verification Teams A, B or C in rotation.

2.2 The verifier checks policy coverage on the date of loss, the assessed amount against the survey or adjudication, KYC and bank details, and any fraud indicators.

2.3 Verification should be completed within 1 working day of assessment.

## 3. QA review

3.1 Every verified claim is reviewed by the QA Desk within 4 working hours.

3.2 QA rejects a claim back to the verifying team when it finds:
- coverage not checked against the date of loss,
- wrong application of deductibles or depreciation,
- KYC or bank details not validated,
- missing verification notes.

## 4. Rework

4.1 **A rejected claim goes back to the originating team's queue.** It is not prioritised over new work, so each rejection typically adds one to two working days to the claim.

4.2 The verifier must record the correction and resubmit to QA.

## 5. Quality targets

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| QA rejection rate per team | **5% or lower** |
| Second rejection of the same claim | below 1% |

5.1 A team whose QA rejection rate is **above 10% for two consecutive months** must receive targeted retraining, and its new joiners must have 100% peer review of their verifications for 30 days.

5.2 Rejection reasons are reported monthly by team.
