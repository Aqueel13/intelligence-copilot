# Motor Survey and Loss Assessment Procedure

Kestrel General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional)

## 1. Purpose

Describes how a surveyor is appointed and how vehicle damage is assessed for motor claims.

## 2. Surveyor appointment

2.1 After the document check, a surveyor licensed by IRDAI is appointed through the Surveyor Network **within 1 working day**.

2.2 Where the claim amount requires it under the Insurance Act, the survey must be done by a licensed surveyor and loss assessor; below that amount an in-house assessor or video survey may be used.

## 3. Survey

3.1 The surveyor inspects the vehicle at the garage or by video survey, checks the damage against the accident description, and agrees the repair estimate with the garage.

3.2 The surveyor applies depreciation on parts as per policy terms and records the assessed loss.

3.3 The survey report must be uploaded within 1 working day of the inspection.

## 4. Re-inspection

A re-inspection may be requested if the final invoice differs from the agreed estimate by more than 10%. Re-inspections must be scheduled within 1 working day.
